# Kavach: 3-minute pitch script

About 430 words, about 3 minutes at a calm pace. Short lines are meant to be said in one breath.
*Italics* are stage directions.

---

## 0:00 – Hook (20 s)

Meet Kaushik. He's seventy-two.
His phone rings. The caller says, "This is your bank's fraud team."
They sound calm. Official. Urgent.
They just need the code he got by text.

That's how millions of people lose money every year.
Not because they're careless. Because scammers are good.

## 0:20 – What Kavach is (25 s)

We built **Kavach**. It means "armor" in Sanskrit.

Kavach turns every scam one person reports into a warning for everyone else's family.

And the main way you use it is something you already know.
**You just text it.**

## 0:45 – Demo: the call (40 s)

*Show the incoming call.*

Before Kaushik even answers, his phone says:
**"Likely scam. 75 reports."**
Seventy-five people already reported this number.

Say he picks up anyway. He opens Call Companion.
It shows three simple rules: don't share codes, ask for the family code word, hang up and call back.

At the same moment, his grandson Raagdeep gets a text:
"Your grandfather asked for help during a call. Reply OK."

Raagdeep replies OK, and Kaushik's screen shows: **Seen.**

And if nobody replies? Kavach reminds the family, and tells Kaushik exactly who to call.

## 1:25 – Demo: iMessage (35 s)

*Show the iMessage thread.*

Now Kaushik gets a text: "Click this link and get a million dollars."
He sends it to Kavach.
In seconds: **"Likely scam. Don't click the link."**

He can also just ask a question.
"My nephew wants gift cards for an emergency. Should I?"
Kavach answers like a calm friend: "Please don't. Call him on a number you already know."

Or he texts: "verify Raagdeep." Kavach asks the real Raagdeep.
Raagdeep says "not me," and the whole family is warned.

## 2:00 – How it works (35 s)

Three things make this work.

**NVIDIA Nemotron** is the brain.
It reads the message, checks it against what the community has reported, and writes a reply anyone can understand.

**SpacetimeDB** is the memory.
It holds the reports, the family, and the rules.
Rules decide the risk level, not the AI, and the AI can never call a known scam safe.

**Photon** is the voice.
It puts Kavach in iMessage, with SMS for family who aren't on iPhone.

## 2:35 – Why it matters (25 s)

T-Mobile asked us to protect customers in ways no one has thought of yet.

Scam detection is only the start.
Kavach also brings in the family, checks that they actually saw the alert, and turns every report into protection for the next person.

Kavach. Armor for the people you love.

Thank you.
